# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Small organisation owners, IT generalists, managed service consultants, and security-minded operators who need a guided way to understand cloud security posture without running an enterprise GRC program.

## Product Purpose

CloudGuard Lite helps users create an organisation profile, complete a practical security self-assessment, run demo or real cloud posture checks, review risk findings, map those findings to internal controls, generate editable internal policies, and export a concise report.

## Brand Personality

Calm, practical, credible. The product should feel like a compact operating console for security work, not a compliance authority or a sales site.

## Anti-references

Avoid heavy enterprise GRC language, certification claims, neon cyber aesthetics, decorative dashboards, generic SaaS hero templates, and interfaces that hide the work behind marketing copy.

## Design Principles

- Show posture through evidence, gaps, and next actions.
- Keep workflows linear enough for non-specialists, while still exposing the underlying records.
- Treat demo mode as a first-class path so reviewers can evaluate the product without cloud credentials.
- Use compliance language carefully: internal control mapping and recommended actions, not legal assurance.
- Prefer dense, calm operational screens over promotional layouts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-usable controls. Avoid relying on color alone for severity or status, and keep motion subtle and tied to state changes.
